# 👑 Q-Empire Social Autopilot

**AI-Powered Social Media Marketing That Runs While You Sleep**

## What This Does

Q-Empire Social Autopilot uses AI to automatically generate unique, platform-optimized social media content for up to **25 different platforms** — all from just your website URL.

## How To Use

1. **Enter your website URL** — The AI analyzes your brand voice, products, and messaging
2. **Select your platforms** — Choose from 25 supported social media platforms
3. **Generate content** — AI creates unique, tailored posts for each platform
4. **Deploy on autopilot** — Schedule automatic posting while you sleep

## Supported Platforms (25)

Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, Reddit, Threads, Tumblr, Medium, Mastodon, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp Business, Snapchat, Bluesky, WordPress, Blogger, Mix, Quora, VK, Weibo, LINE, KakaoTalk

## Features

- 🤖 AI-powered content generation with platform-specific optimization
- 📱 25 platform support with unique content per platform
- ⏰ Autopilot scheduling (daily, weekly, custom)
- 📊 Engagement scoring and analytics
- 🔔 Automated owner notifications

## 1. Buffer

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Founded** | 2010 |
| **Pricing** | Free (3 channels) → $6/mo → $12/mo → $120/mo (Agency) |
| **Users** | 7M+ worldwide |
| **Platforms** | 8 major (FB, IG, X, LI, Pinterest, TikTok, Mastodon, GMB) |

### Strengths
- Clean, intuitive UI — best-in-class ease of use
- Strong free tier (3 channels, 10 scheduled posts)
- Excellent browser extension and mobile app
- Transparent, simple pricing
- Pablo image creator built-in

### Weaknesses
- No AI content generation (only AI Assistant in top tier)
- Limited to 8 platforms (no Discord, Telegram, Reddit, Bluesky)
- No autopilot / auto-scheduling intelligence
- Analytics are basic compared to Sprout Social
- No website analysis or brand voice extraction

### How Q-Empire Beats Buffer
- **25 platforms** vs Buffer's 8 — including emerging platforms (Bluesky, Threads, Discord, Telegram)
- **Real AI content generation** with Mistral-7B — not just an assistant
- **Website analysis** extracts brand voice automatically
- **Autopilot mode** auto-schedules optimal times
- **4-tier pricing** with a genuinely useful free tier (5 posts/day, 3 platforms)

| Attribute | Detail |

| **Pricing** | $99/mo → $249/mo → $739/mo (Enterprise) |
| **Users** | 18M+ |
| **Platforms** | 10+ (including YouTube, Reddit) |

### Strengths
- Enterprise-grade social listening and monitoring

### How Q-Empire Beats Hootsuite
- **Free tier** with real functionality vs Hootsuite's $99 entry point
- **AI-native architecture** — not bolted-on templates
- **Simpler UX** — no 50-tab dashboard
- **Price**: Q-Empire Pro ($49.99) has comparable features to Hootsuite Team ($249)
- **Website analysis** — Hootsuite has no equivalent feature
